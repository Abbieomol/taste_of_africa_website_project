What's cooking

Latest Food Trends

15 Oct 2026 · Health

Traditional greens are back on the menu

Young Nairobi chefs are rediscovering indigenous vegetables such as managu, terere and saga. Once dismissed as "village food", they are now prized for being nutritious, climate-hardy and full of flavour.

28 Sep 2026 · Grains

Ancient grains go global

West Africa's fonio and Ethiopia's teff are showing up in bakeries and cafés around the world. Both are gluten-free, quick to cook and have been grown on the continent for thousands of years.

10 Sep 2026 · Culture

Swahili coast flavours inspire fusion kitchens

The Indian Ocean spice trade shaped dishes like pilau and biryani on the coast. Today, restaurants are mixing pilau masala and coconut into burgers, tacos and pastries for a new generation of diners.
