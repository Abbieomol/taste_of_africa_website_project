Chicken Tagine

Slow-Braised Moroccan Stew

Chicken slow-braised with preserved lemon, olives and saffron in Morocco's iconic cone-shaped clay pot.

  • Total time1 hr 30 min
  • Serves4
  • DifficultyMedium

The story behind the dish

A tagine is both the dish and the pot it is cooked in: a shallow clay base topped with a tall cone-shaped lid that traps steam and drips it back onto the food. This lets meat slow-braise in very little water — ideal in a dry climate.

Moroccan tagines balance sweet, sour and savoury with preserved lemon, green olives, honey or dried fruit, and spice blends like ras el hanout, saffron, cumin and cinnamon — flavours shaped by Amazigh (Berber), Arab and Andalusian traditions.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken thighs or pieces
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 onions, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp ginger, ground
  • 1 tsp cumin, ground
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • A pinch of saffron threads
  • 1 preserved lemon, rind sliced
  • ½ cup green olives
  • 1 cup water
  • Fresh coriander and parsley to garnish

Serve with: Khobz (Moroccan bread), couscous or a fresh tomato salad.

Cooking Instructions

Tap each step to expand it.

1 Marinate
  1. Mix the garlic, spices, saffron and half the oil and rub over the chicken. Leave for 30 minutes (or overnight).
2 Layer the tagine
  1. Spread the grated onion and remaining oil in the base of a tagine or heavy pot.
  2. Arrange the chicken on top, add the cinnamon stick and pour in the water.
3 Slow braise
  1. Cover and cook on low heat for 50–60 minutes, until the chicken is very tender and the onions form a thick sauce.
  2. Don't lift the lid too often — the trapped steam does the work.
4 Finish
  1. Add the preserved lemon and olives and cook uncovered for 10 minutes more.
  2. Scatter with herbs and serve with khobz (bread) or couscous.
