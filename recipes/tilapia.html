Kenyan Tilapia

Wet Fry Fish from Lake Victoria

Whole tilapia fried until crisp, then simmered in a tomato and onion sauce — Lake Victoria's favourite meal.

  • Total time45 min
  • Serves2
  • DifficultyMedium

The story behind the dish

Around the shores of Lake Victoria — in Kisumu, Homa Bay and Siaya — fresh tilapia is a daily staple. Known in Dholuo as ngege, it is the pride of Luo cooking and the reason visitors make the trip to Dunga Beach.

"Wet fry" is a uniquely Kenyan technique: the fish is first deep-fried until golden and crisp, then gently simmered in a tomato sauce so it soaks up flavour without falling apart. It is served with ugali, sukuma wiki and kachumbari.

Ingredients

  • 2 whole tilapia, cleaned and scaled
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes, grated or blended
  • 1 green bell pepper (hoho), sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • 1 cup water
  • A handful of dhania (coriander), chopped

Serve with: Ugali, sukuma wiki and kachumbari.

Cooking Instructions

Tap each step to expand it.

1 Season the fish
  1. Make 2–3 shallow cuts on each side of the fish.
  2. Rub with lemon juice and salt and leave for 15 minutes.
2 Fry until golden
  1. Pat the fish dry. Heat the oil until hot and fry each fish for 5–6 minutes per side until crisp.
  2. Drain on paper towels.
3 Make the sauce
  1. In a wide pan, fry the onion with a little oil until soft, then add the garlic and ginger.
  2. Add the tomatoes and hoho and cook into a thick sauce. Add the water and bring to a simmer.
4 Simmer and serve
  1. Lay the fried fish in the sauce, spoon sauce over the top and simmer for 8–10 minutes.
  2. Garnish with dhania and serve with ugali and greens.
